About HEARTIE 💜

Who We Are

HEARTIE is a small, student-run business from Camiling, Tarlac — created with one simple goal: to make beautiful, affordable, and meaningful beaded accessories that you can wear, gift, and cherish. Every bracelet, keychain, and phone charm is made with passion and attention to every single bead!

Our Mission

To create high-quality, handmade beaded accessories that bring joy, color, and personal expression to everyone — while supporting local creativity and craftsmanship.

Our Vision

To be your go-to for cute, customizable, and heartfelt beaded accessories — where every piece tells a story.
